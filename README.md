<h3>Hello there 👋, I'm Allison!</h3>

I'm specifically interested in how AI can see, plan, and act in the real world. I think world models, GUI agents, and vision-language models are all super cool, and I've been exploring them [here](https://github.com/allisonandreyev/Earudite) and [there](https://github.com/allisonandreyev/arxAIv). I also build projects for fun, like [CourseCat](https://github.com/allisonandreyev/coursecat), a Canvas companion app with an AI agent; I'm always working on something new. Outside of AI, I love building assistive robots such as [SignScribe](https://github.com/allisonandreyev/signscribe), [GRASP](https://github.com/allisonandreyev/GRASP), and my newest one, [Nudge V2](https://github.com/allisonandreyev/NudgeV1) (all built in collaboration with UMD CPSE!)

Check out [my personal website](https://allisonandreyev.github.io/)!
